**⛏️ Motherlode Mine — v1.0.2**

Three fixes, all from your reports 🙏

⬥ **Repairing the water wheel no longer gets stuck.** It would climb down, spot the broken strut and then just stand there — it only clicked once you zoomed out manually. It now pulls the camera back itself when something won't fit on screen. Same fix applies to veins and the ladder.

⬥ **Dropped pay-dirt is no longer clicked through the floor.** If it dropped your pay-dirt upstairs, then went down to empty the sack, it would stand downstairs spam-clicking a pile it couldn't reach. It now knows which level the pay-dirt is on and waits until it's back up there before collecting.

⬥ **Pay-dirt pickup is more precise.** It walks to the exact tile it dropped on instead of grabbing whatever pay-dirt is lying nearby.

**Note:** it doesn't make a special trip for the dropped pay-dirt — it picks it up when it heads back to mine. So there's a gap between emptying the sack and collecting. That's intended.

Keep the reports coming 👍
