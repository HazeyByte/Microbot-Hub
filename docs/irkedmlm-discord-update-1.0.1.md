**⛏️ Motherlode Mine — v1.0.1 is up**

Thanks to everyone who ran it. All of these came from your reports 🙏

**Fixed**

⬥ **Hopper spam when the sack filled up** — it would sit there clicking the hopper over and over instead of going to empty the sack. Biggest one by far.
⬥ **Wrong Sack Size no longer breaks the run** — if you had it set to Upgraded but still have the 108 sack (or vice versa), it now works it out on its own instead of getting stuck.
⬥ **Dropped pay-dirt is picked back up** — it drops your pay-dirt to make room for emptying the sack, and now collects it afterwards and deposits it at the hopper before going back to mining. It used to just leave it on the floor.
⬥ **Drop Gems actually drops gems** — the option did nothing at all before. Gems now get dropped the moment they appear and mining carries straight on with the same vein.
⬥ **No more getting stuck with a full inventory** — if your inventory filled up with something other than pay-dirt it could sit there doing nothing.
⬥ **No more `[WebWalk] clear` warnings spamming your chatbox** — that was us, and it was harmless, but it's gone.

**Still worth knowing**

⬥ Start the bot **inside the Motherlode Mine**. Walking there from outside still isn't reliable — that's next on the list.
⬥ If you use a **gem bag**, leave *Drop Gems* off — the bag does the job and dropping is skipped anyway.

**Please keep testing**

Same asks as before, but especially:
⬥ Let the sack fill completely and watch what it does — that's where most of these were hiding
⬥ Mining spots other than West Upper
⬥ Long runs, then check the overlay numbers still look sane

Report anything odd in this thread — mining area, your config, and a screenshot with the overlay visible if you can 👍
